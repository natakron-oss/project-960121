Magic Cake

Magic Cake is one the tastiest cakes the world has ever know. Few have tried it and lived to tell about it, and these are their stories.

We also need to strees that whatever happens to you as a result of consuming Magic Cake is your responsibility. You've been warned, and you can view our legal page. for more information

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1 pound of sugar
  • 3 sticks of butter

Directions

  1. Mix eggs, sugar, and butter in a large bowl.
  2. Spread into non-stick dish
  3. Bake at 350 degress for 1 hour
  4. let sit at the room temperature for 20 minutes
  5. Eat and enjoy!
